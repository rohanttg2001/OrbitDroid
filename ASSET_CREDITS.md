## Supplied screenshots and PDF

The four PNGs in `assets/screenshots/` are the supplied earlier Community dashboard,
Android session, Google's Extended Controls and macOS warning. The first three
show earlier builds. Apps shown inside Android were installed separately and
are not bundled. The dashboard's earlier open-source wording is labelled historical.

`assets/guides/open-anyway.pdf` preserves the supplied annotated settings PDF;
`open-anyway.png` is its rendered preview. System prompts can vary by macOS version.
`assets/logo.svg` is the existing OrbitDroid orbital mark.

Apple, Android, Google and depicted third-party app names/icons belong to their
owners. Their appearance implies no endorsement, affiliation or redistribution
of those apps. The reference screenshot of API Anything was used only to clarify
the README layout requested; it is not included in this public package.

## Decorative emerald artwork

`assets/emerald-orbit.webp` is AI-generated decorative concept art, shown in the
README's optional artwork panel. Mode: **generate**, using the built-in image
generation tool, then converted to WebP. It is not a product screenshot.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape hero background for the OrbitDroid Android player for Mac public GitHub guide. Create an exquisite cinematic emerald aurora over a still alpine lake at night, almost-black pine and mountain silhouettes, fine stars and atmospheric mist. A luminous mint orbital ring and a subtle glassy emerald planet float over the lake on the RIGHT third, suggesting Android apps entering orbit; the ring feels dimensional and softly radiant rather than a flat vector. Deep ink-green left half with gentle low-detail texture and generous negative space for separately rendered website headings. Neon emerald and cool mint light reflected on dark water, tiny restrained hints of teal. Premium desktop software editorial aesthetic, photoreal landscape with surreal orbital light, rich nuanced blacks and luminous fine detail. Wide 16:9 composition. No UI, no laptop, no people, no letters, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is an atmospheric background asset, not a screenshot and not an infographic.
